On July 12, 2026, Yoruba achieved a 6.26 ZZ World Record ao5 at [Cube4fun Lublin Summer 2026](https://www.worldcubeassociation.org/competitions/Cube4funLublinSummer2026).
He broke his [6.51 ZZ WR](/en/example-solves/yoruba-6.51-ao5) from almost exactly a year ago.
This average was ranked 8th in Poland, and is ranked 10th at the time of posting this article.
