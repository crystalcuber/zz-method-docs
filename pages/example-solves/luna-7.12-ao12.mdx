## Self-Critique

Main issue was that F2L was inconsistent and I need to touch up some ZBLL.

### EOCross

Average EOCross+1 movecount here was 14.42 (very good), but again, the scrambles were quite lucky.
51.17 average movecount is also crazy good.

### F2L

F2L lookahead in these solves was pretty hit or miss - some solves F2L was really smooth, others had a lot of bad pauses.
I just need to remind myself every solve to focus on not pausing in F2L rather than just turning fast.
Planned 1st pair on quite a few of these, if I learn to do it every time I think I can definitely global sub-7.

### ZBLL

ZBLLs were pretty fast but those cases were definitely above average.
